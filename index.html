The Firm

Three decades before the Bar. One standard of care.

Baath & Associates is a chambers practice built around the work of Bikramjit Singh Baath, former Deputy Advocate General, Punjab — from trial courts across Punjab and Haryana to the High Court at Chandigarh and the Supreme Court of India.

The firm's philosophy is unfashionably simple: preparation decides cases. Every brief is read in full, every precedent traced to its source, every weakness in a client's position identified in conference — long before an opponent finds it in court.
